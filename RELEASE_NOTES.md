First release: 255 round caps for Wunder-Bar M4 bar guns.

- 112 labels from the commercial Wunder-Bar round cap catalogue (single letters, COKE, TONIC, DIET, ...).
- 143 more drinks: spirits, mixers, juices, syrups and cocktails.
- Each 3MF is one object with two parts, `Cap` and `Label` (0.4 mm inlay). Print the face down.
- Open `round/index.html` in the archive to see every cap.
