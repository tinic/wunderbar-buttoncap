Adds a storage tray and the order sheet.

- `storage/CapTray.3mf`: a tray for a card box with a 200 x 200 mm inside. Caps sit label up on pegs shaped like the gun's button and lift straight off: 210 round caps and, in the front row, 8 long caps per tray. Trays stack, and notches in two walls let you lift a tray out.
- `storage/CapTray-fit-coupon.3mf`: a corner of the tray with 6 round pegs and a long peg. Print it first to check the fit.
- `order-sheet.pdf`: every cap with a checkbox and a quantity field, US Letter, for a bar to tick the caps it wants.
- Caps: the same 458 round and 26 long caps as v0.8.0.
