Labels now use short codes, as on the original caps: PJ for pickle juice, JAVA for coffee, CAMP for Campari.

- 227 round caps: the 112 labels of the commercial Wunder-Bar catalogue, in the vendor colours, and 115 house codes.
- The 3MF files have the drink's name (`PICKLE_JUICE.3mf`); the cap has the code (PJ).
- House caps have their own colours, chosen for contrast on a 10 mm cap.
- Drinks that a catalogue cap already covers (GALE, MTDEW, BMARY, ...) no longer have a second house cap.
- Open `round/index.html` in the archive to see every cap, grouped.
